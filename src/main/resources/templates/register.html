E-Vote System � Voter Registration Terms and Conditions

Last updated: January 2025


1. Eligibility

You must be a Filipino citizen, at least 18 years of age, and a resident of Laguna province to register as a voter in this system. By registering, you confirm that you meet all eligibility requirements under Philippine election law.


2. Accuracy of Information

You agree to provide accurate, complete, and truthful information during registration. Providing false information is a violation of the Omnibus Election Code and may result in criminal liability. The E-Vote system reserves the right to verify all submitted information.


3. Privacy and Data Protection

Your personal information, including name, date of birth, address, contact details, government ID, and biometric data (selfie), will be collected and processed in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Your data will be used solely for voter verification and election administration purposes.


4. One Account Per Voter

Each eligible voter is permitted to create only one account. Creating multiple accounts is strictly prohibited and may result in disqualification and legal action.


5. Account Security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. Do not share your password with anyone. The E-Vote system will never ask for your password via email or phone. Report any unauthorized access immediately.


6. OTP Verification

A One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email address for identity verification. This OTP is valid for 10 minutes and must not be shared with anyone.


7. ID and Face Verification

You consent to the collection and processing of your government-issued ID photo and facial image for identity verification purposes. These images will be reviewed by authorized administrators and stored securely.


8. Voting Rights

Registered voters may cast one vote per election. Attempting to vote multiple times, manipulating the system, or interfering with the voting process is a criminal offense under Philippine law.


9. System Availability

The E-Vote system strives for maximum uptime during election periods. However, we do not guarantee uninterrupted service. Technical issues should be reported to the system administrator immediately.


10. Amendments

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of any revised terms.


11. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, including but not limited to the Omnibus Election Code, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and applicable COMELEC regulations.
