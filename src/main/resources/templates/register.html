E-Vote System — Voter Registration Terms and Conditions

By registering as a voter in the E-Vote System, you agree to the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before proceeding.

1. Eligibility
You must be a Filipino citizen, at least 18 years of age, and a resident of the municipality or city where you are registering. You must not be disqualified from voting under existing laws.

2. Accuracy of Information
You certify that all information provided during registration is true, accurate, and complete. Providing false information is a violation of election laws and may result in criminal liability.

3. One Registration Per Voter
Each eligible voter may only register once. Duplicate registrations are prohibited and will be rejected. Any attempt to register multiple times may result in disqualification.

4. Privacy and Data Protection
Your personal information will be collected, stored, and processed in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Your data will only be used for voter registration and election purposes.

5. ID Verification
You consent to the submission and verification of your government-issued ID. The system may use facial recognition technology to verify your identity against your submitted ID photo.

6. Account Security
You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Do not share your username or password with anyone. Report any unauthorized access immediately.

7. Voting Integrity
You agree to cast your vote honestly and independently. Vote buying, selling, or any form of electoral fraud is strictly prohibited and punishable by law.

8. System Use
You agree to use the E-Vote System only for its intended purpose. Any attempt to manipulate, hack, or disrupt the system is a criminal offense.

9. Approval Process
Registration is subject to review and approval by the election administrator. You will be notified of the status of your registration.

10. Amendments
These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of any changes.

By clicking "I Agree", you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions.
