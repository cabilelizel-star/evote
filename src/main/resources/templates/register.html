Terms & Conditions / Privacy Policy

1. Eligibility

By registering, you confirm that you are a qualified voter and all information provided is accurate, complete, and truthful. Providing false information is a violation of this agreement.

2. One Vote Per Voter

Each registered voter may cast exactly one (1) vote per election. Multiple voting attempts are strictly prohibited and may result in disqualification.

3. Identity Verification

You consent to identity verification using your uploaded valid ID and selfie photo. This data is used solely for voter identity verification and will not be shared with third parties.

4. Confidentiality of Credentials

You are responsible for keeping your Voter ID and password confidential. Do not share your login credentials with anyone.

5. Data Privacy (R.A. 10173)

Your personal information is collected and processed in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. It is used solely for voter verification and election administration purposes. You have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data.

6. Election Integrity

Any attempt to manipulate, tamper with, or interfere with the voting process — including hacking, impersonation, or coercion — is strictly prohibited and may be subject to legal action under applicable laws.

7. Finality of Vote

Once your vote has been cast and confirmed, it is final and cannot be changed or withdrawn. Please review your choice carefully before submitting.

8. OTP Verification

An OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to your registered email address to verify your identity. This OTP expires in 10 minutes and must not be shared with anyone.

9. Acceptance

By clicking "I Agree", you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
