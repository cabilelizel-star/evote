📜 Rules & Guidelines
Voting Instructions

How to Vote

  • Go to the Vote section from the sidebar.
  • Review all candidates carefully before making your choice.
  • Click on a candidate card to select them.
  • Click Cast My Vote and confirm your selection.
  • Your vote is final and cannot be changed once submitted.

Election Rules

  • Each registered voter may cast exactly one (1) vote per election.
  • Voting is only allowed during the official election period when status is OPEN.
  • Any attempt to vote multiple times or impersonate another voter is strictly prohibited.
  • Violations may result in disqualification and legal action.

Ballot Secrecy

Your vote is completely secret. The system records only that you voted — not who you voted for. This is guaranteed by the design of the E-Vote System.

Privacy Notice (R.A. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Your personal information is collected and processed solely for voter verification and election administration. It is protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. You have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data by contacting the election administrator.

Technical Support

If you encounter any issues during voting, please contact the election administrator immediately. Do not attempt to vote again until the issue is resolved.
