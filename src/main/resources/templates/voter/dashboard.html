📜 Rules & Guidelines
🗳 Voting Instructions
  • 1️⃣ Each registered voter is entitled to cast one vote only per election. Votes cannot be changed once submitted.
  • 2️⃣ Navigate to the Vote section and select your preferred candidate by clicking their card.
  • 3️⃣ Review your selection carefully before pressing Cast My Vote. A confirmation prompt will appear.
  • 4️⃣ Voting is only available while the election is Open. Check the status badge in the top navigation bar.
  • 5️⃣ Your vote is secret and anonymous. No one, including administrators, can see who you voted for.
⚠ Important Reminders
  • 🔒 Keep your login credentials confidential. Do not share your Voter ID or password with anyone.
  • 📵 Do not attempt to vote multiple times. Any fraudulent activity will result in disqualification.
  • 📧 A confirmation email will be sent to your registered email address after your vote is successfully cast.
  • 📞 If you encounter any issues, contact the election administrator immediately.
  • ✅ Ensure your profile information is up to date, especially your email address, to receive important notifications.
⚖ Voter Rights
  • 🤝 Every eligible voter has the right to cast their vote freely and without coercion.
  • 📊 Live results are available to all voters once the election closes.
  • 🛡 Your personal data is protected and used solely for election purposes.
